Location & setting

Life at by Long Hill Residence

A residential quarter set in twenty-five thousand square metres of preserved forest at Mjull Bathore, five minutes from central Tirana and one minute from TEG. Vehicles are kept entirely underground, so the ground plane belongs to the residents; rooftop solar offsets the residence's electricity cost, and reception, security and maintenance run around the clock.
